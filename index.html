NZB Control

A simple status bar app to monitor and control your SABnzbd or NZBGet instance. Perfect for setups on a NAS or remote server.

Real-Time Monitoring

Track download speed, remaining time, and queue size at a glance from your Mac's menu bar. Stay informed without leaving your workflow.

Queue Management

View the latest 5 items in your queue and history. Pause, resume, delete, rename, adjust priority, post-processing, category, or scripts with ease.

Speed Control

Set download speed limits directly from the app. Fine-tune bandwidth on the fly to match your network conditions.

Seamless NZB Addition

Add NZB files via URL or drag-and-drop upload from your Mac. Full NZB file association support for instant integration.

Effortless Control

Everything accessible from a clean status bar menu. No clutter, just precise control over your downloads.

How It Works

1. Connect

Enter your SABnzbd or NZBGet server details once. The app connects securely and stays in your menu bar.

2. Monitor

Watch live stats: speed, time remaining, queue size. Glanceable updates keep you in control.

3. Manage

Pause/resume queue, tweak individual items, limit speeds, or add NZBs. All from one menu.

4. Done

Your downloads run smoothly on the server while you stay productive on Mac.
